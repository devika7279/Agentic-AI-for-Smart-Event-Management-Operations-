Privacy at EventOps AI

We value your trust. This page explains the types of information our event management platform may use, why it is needed, and how privacy should be maintained.

01. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, information may include:

  • Name and email address
  • Event registration and attendance details
  • Speaker and sponsorship information
  • Messages submitted through the contact form

02. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to manage registrations, organize events, coordinate speakers and venues, provide notifications, and improve platform services.

03. Data Security

Appropriate security measures should be used to protect stored information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

04. Information Sharing

Information should only be shared when necessary for platform operations, with appropriate authorization, or when required by applicable law.

05. Cookies and Sessions

The platform may use session cookies to support login and navigation. These help maintain your session while you use the website.

06. Your Privacy Choices

You can contact the platform administrator with questions about your information or requests for corrections, subject to applicable requirements.

07. Policy Updates

This policy may change as EventOps AI develops. Any updated policy will be published on this page.

08. Questions About Privacy?

If you have questions or concerns about privacy, please contact us.
